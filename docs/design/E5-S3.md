# E5-S3 Dashboard (Dasbor)

## Task

At the start of the day, or between visits, the Admin sees how the team stands against this month's Target and what needs attention today.

## Priority of content

1. Team Realisasi against Target this month (the one memorable element: a large figure)
2. Overdue items (Terlambat)
3. Today's Kegiatan
4. Each Funding Officer's Realisasi against Target
5. Open Pipelines by Stage
6. Next 7 days' Kegiatan
7. The month picker

Sections are separated by borders and 24 px spacing, not by a grid of identical cards.

**Tablet (768):** two columns: left Realisasi tim and Per Funding Officer; right Terlambat and Kegiatan hari ini; full width below for Pipeline per tahap and 7 hari ke depan.

**Desktop (1280):** a top band across the full width: Realisasi tim on the left (the 28 px figure, the bar, last month) and the Per Funding Officer table on the right (Funding Officer, Realisasi, Target, %, Bulan lalu; tabular, right-aligned numbers). Below, three columns: Terlambat, Kegiatan hari ini and 7 hari ke depan, Pipeline per tahap.

Every overdue item, Kegiatan and Stage row is a link: an overdue Task opens its Pipeline; a Kegiatan opens its Activity detail; a Stage row opens the Prospect list filtered by that Stage (see the backlog open question on the Stage filter). This keeps any Pipeline within 3 taps.

## States

- **First use (EC1):** instead of the sections, a short ordered checklist (a real sequence, so numbered): "1. Tambahkan Funding Officer", "2. Atur Target bulan ini", "3. Tambahkan Prospect pertama". Each step links to its screen and shows "Selesai" with a check once done. The checklist stays until all three are done.
- **No Target for the month (EC4):** Realisasi shows its figure with "Target belum diatur" and the link "Atur Target", and no bar.
- **A past or future month:** "Kegiatan hari ini" and "7 hari ke depan" are hidden; the others show that month.
- **Nothing overdue:** "Tidak ada yang terlambat." (one line, no illustration).
- **Loading:** skeletons per section.
- **Error:** per section: "Data dasbor gagal dimuat." with "Coba lagi".
- **Target reached:** the bar turns success with "Tercapai".

## Edge cases

- EC4, EC1: as above.
- EC30: overdue items with "Terlambat" and `Clock`.
- EC44: numbers with `id-ID` grouping; percentages rounded down to whole numbers ("69%").
- E1-S4: inactive Funding Officers appear in "Per Funding Officer" only if they have a Target or Realisasi that month, with the label.

## Copy

Title "Dasbor". Sections "Realisasi tim", "Terlambat", "Kegiatan hari ini", "Per Funding Officer", "Pipeline berjalan per tahap", "7 hari ke depan". "dari 1.800 target", "Bulan lalu", "Target belum diatur", "Atur Target", "Tercapai", "Tampilkan semua" (expands the list in place; the first 5 items show by default). Month stepper labels "Bulan sebelumnya" / "Bulan berikutnya".

## Components and tokens used

shadcn `Progress`, `Table`, `Skeleton`, `Button`. `StageBadge` position numbers in "Pipeline per tahap". Tokens `--primary` (bar), `--success` (reached), `--warning` (Terlambat). Tabular figures everywhere numbers appear.

## Accessibility notes

- The progress bars have `aria-valuenow`, `aria-valuemax` and a text equivalent ("69 persen dari target").
- Section headings are `h2`, giving a clear outline for screen readers.
