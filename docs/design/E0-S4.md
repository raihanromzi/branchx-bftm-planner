# E0-S4 App shell

## Task

The Admin (BFTM) moves between the main screens in one tap, on a phone in the field or on a laptop at the branch, and can install the app to the home screen.

## Priority of content

1. The current screen's content (the shell takes as little space as possible)
2. Navigation to Dashboard, Prospect, Jadwal and Peta (the daily destinations)
3. Page title, and a back control on detail pages
4. Pengaturan and Keluar (rare)

## Layout

**Mobile (360) and tablet (768):** a top bar 56 px high with the page title (20 px semibold, left-aligned) and, on detail pages, a back arrow on the left. Content scrolls under it. A bottom navigation bar of 64 px plus `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)` holds five equal items (72 px each at 360), each a 24 px icon above a 14 px medium label. "Prospect" is the longest label and fits.

**Desktop (1280):** a left sidebar 240 px wide, full height, on `--surface`. From the top: the product name "BFTM Planner" in 16 px semibold, then the nav items (icon + label, 40 px rows), a "Pengaturan" group with its three sub-items, and at the bottom the signed-in email (14 px muted) and "Keluar". The content area has a 24 px gutter and a page header row with the title (28 px semibold) and the page's primary action on the right.

## States

- **Active item:** the accent text colour, a 600 weight, and an indicator: a 3 px bar on the left in the sidebar, or a filled icon with an `--accent` pill behind it in the bottom nav. Never colour alone.
- **Loading:** the shell renders immediately; only the content area shows skeletons.
- **Signing out:** "Keluar" becomes disabled with "Keluar…" until the redirect.

## Edge cases

- Fixed bars never cover content: the content area has bottom padding equal to the nav height plus the safe area (DESIGN.md responsive rules).
- A long page title (a 150-character Prospect name) is truncated to one line in the top bar; the full name appears as the page's first heading below.

## Copy

Nav: "Dasbor", "Prospect", "Jadwal", "Peta", "Lainnya". Sidebar group: "Pengaturan", with "Funding Officer", "Template Pipeline", "Target". "Keluar". Back control `aria-label`: "Kembali".

## Components and tokens used

shadcn `Sheet` (Lainnya on mobile), `Button`, `Separator`. lucide: `LayoutDashboard`, `Building2`, `CalendarDays`, `Map`, `Ellipsis`, `Settings`, `LogOut`, `ArrowLeft`. Tokens: `--surface`, `--accent`, `--primary`, `--ring`. Font: Plus Jakarta Sans via `next/font` on `<html>`. The manifest uses `--primary` `#0E6E73` as `theme_color` and `#FFFFFF` as `background_color`. The icons are a white teal-backed "BP" monogram at 192 and 512 px, plus a maskable version.

Shared format helpers (`src/lib/format.ts`): `formatNumber(1250)` → "1.250"; `formatDate` → "18 Nov 2026"; `formatTime` → "14.30 WIB"; `formatDateTime` → "18 Nov 2026, 14.30 WIB"; `formatMonth` → "November 2026". All in `Asia/Jakarta`.

## Accessibility notes

- `<nav aria-label="Navigasi utama">`; the active item has `aria-current="page"`.
- Every bottom-nav target is at least 64 × 56 px.
- A "Lewati ke konten" skip link is the first focusable element on desktop.
- The focus ring is 2 px `--ring` with a 2 px offset on every item.
