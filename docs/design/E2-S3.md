# E2-S3 Prospect form (add and edit)

## Task

The Admin adds a Prospect in the field in under a minute, or edits one, on a phone with one hand.

## Priority of content

1. Nama (the only required field besides Funding Officer)
2. Funding Officer, Kategori
3. Lokasi (GPS in one tap)
4. Kontak
5. Alamat, Potensi rekening, Catatan

## Layout

A full page (`/prospect/baru`, `/prospect/[id]/ubah`), one column, label above each field, errors under the field. The submit bar is sticky at the bottom on mobile.

**Tablet (768):** the same column, max width 640 px; Kontak's three fields stay stacked.

**Desktop (1280):** two columns inside a 960 px form: left column Nama, Funding Officer, Kategori, Alamat, Kontak, Potensi rekening, Catatan; right column Lokasi with a 360 px-high map. "Simpan" and "Batal" sit at the bottom left, not sticky.

## States

- **Normal (new):** empty, Funding Officer preselected when there is only one active.
- **Normal (edit):** prefilled; the title is "Ubah Prospect".
- **Saving:** "Simpan" disabled with "Menyimpan…".
- **Saved:** go to the Prospect detail with a toast "Prospect disimpan."
- **Loading (edit):** a skeleton of the form.

## Edge cases

- EC2: no active Funding Officers: the form is replaced by "Tambahkan Funding Officer dulu. Setiap Prospect perlu ditugaskan ke satu Funding Officer." with a link "Buka Pengaturan Funding Officer".
- EC9: "Nama Prospect wajib diisi" under Nama; focus moves to the first error.
- EC10: "Potensi rekening harus bilangan bulat 0 sampai 100.000."
- EC11: phone error from the server under Telepon.
- EC12, EC42: the counter "0/500" and the hint under Catatan; a server rejection shows "Jangan tulis NIK atau nomor rekening nasabah." under Catatan.
- EC13: "Lokasi di luar Indonesia." under the map.
- EC14: after a save with a matching name, a warning panel at the top (warning colour, `TriangleAlert`): "Sudah ada Prospect bernama SDN 3 Cibinong" with a link to each match, and "Tetap simpan" / "Batal". The save is not repeated automatically.
- EC15: the button is disabled while saving; the Idempotency-Key is created once per form open.
- EC18: a 409 shows the reload message in an alert at the top with "Muat ulang"; inputs are kept.
- EC33: GPS denied: "Izin lokasi ditolak. Ketuk peta untuk menandai lokasi." GPS slow (15 s): "Lokasi belum didapat. Ketuk peta untuk menandai lokasi." The button shows "Mencari lokasi…" while waiting.
- EC36: "Gagal menyimpan. Periksa koneksi lalu coba lagi." with "Coba lagi"; all inputs kept.

## Copy

Titles "Tambah Prospect" / "Ubah Prospect". Labels as in the sketch; "*" marks required, with "* wajib diisi" once at the top. Kategori options: Sekolah, Pasar, Perusahaan, Instansi, Komunitas, Lainnya. "Gunakan lokasi saya", "Hapus lokasi". Buttons "Simpan", "Batal", "Tetap simpan".

## Components and tokens used

shadcn `Input`, `Textarea`, `Select`, `Label`, `Button`, `Alert`, `Sonner` toast. A `LocationPicker` component (react-leaflet, one draggable marker, OSM tiles), loaded client-side only, shared with no other screen. lucide `LocateFixed`, `TriangleAlert`.

## Accessibility notes

- Errors are linked to inputs with `aria-describedby` and `aria-invalid`.
- `inputmode="numeric"` for Potensi rekening, `type="tel"` for Telepon.
- The map is optional for keyboard users: the latitude and longitude can also be typed into two inputs under a "Masukkan koordinat" disclosure.
