# E3-S4 Pipeline detail

## Task

Right after a visit, the Admin ticks the Tasks that were done and moves the Pipeline to its next Stage, or closes it with an Outcome, in a few taps on a phone.

## Priority of content

1. The current Stage and its Tasks (tick in one tap)
2. "Pindah ke tahap berikutnya"
3. Prospect name and the Pipeline's Funding Officer
4. Set the Outcome
5. Other Stages and their Tasks
6. Change Funding Officer, reopen

Tasks are rows of 48 px with a checkbox on the left; the due date (if any) under the name with "Ubah tanggal" in the row's menu. Tapping a Stage in "Semua tahap" expands its Tasks inline and shows "Pindah ke tahap ini".

**Tablet (768):** the same, max width 720 px.

**Desktop (1280):** header with the Prospect name (link), the Stage badge, Funding Officer and the actions on the right: "Pindah ke Negosiasi" (primary), "Tandai berhasil", and a menu (Tunda, Tandai gagal, Ganti Funding Officer). Below, a horizontal stepper of all Stages (numbers and names, current one highlighted with weight and the accent bar) and, under it, two columns: the current Stage's Tasks (left, 2/3) and a side panel with Prospect, Funding Officer, template, started date and the linked Kegiatan (right, 1/3).

## States

- **Open:** as above.
- **On Hold:** an "Ditunda" outcome badge in the header; Tasks are read-only; actions "Lanjutkan" and "Tandai gagal".
- **Won:** "Berhasil" badge; a summary "350 rekening dibuka, 18 Nov 2026"; action "Buka kembali".
- **Lost:** "Gagal" badge with the reason; action "Buka kembali".
- **Loading:** skeleton of the header and the Task list.
- **Error:** "Pipeline gagal dimuat." with "Coba lagi". Not found: "Pipeline tidak ditemukan." with a link to the Prospect list.
- **Task tick fails:** the checkbox reverts and a toast says "Tugas gagal disimpan. Coba lagi."

## Edge cases

- EC23: moving forward with unticked Tasks opens a confirm: "3 Tugas belum selesai. Tetap pindah ke Presentasi?" ("Pindah" / "Batal"). Moving back has no warning. Closed or On Hold Pipelines show no move actions.
- EC24: "Tandai berhasil" opens a dialog / sheet with "Rekening dibuka" (number) and "Tanggal berhasil" (date, default today, max today). "Tandai gagal" asks "Alasan" (textarea with counter and the customer-data hint). "Tunda" asks for confirmation only.
- EC10, EC32: "Rekening dibuka harus bilangan bulat 1 sampai 100.000." "Tanggal berhasil tidak boleh di masa depan."
- EC25: "Buka kembali" on Won: "Buka kembali Pipeline ini? Realisasi November 2026 untuk Rina Kartika berkurang 350 rekening." On Lost: "Buka kembali Pipeline ini? Ia kembali ke tahap Presentasi." If the Prospect has another open Pipeline, the action is disabled with the reason "Prospect ini sudah punya Pipeline berjalan."
- EC26: "Ganti Funding Officer" opens a select of active Funding Officers; the note says "Realisasi Pipeline ini dihitung untuk Funding Officer yang dipilih."
- EC30: "Terlambat" with `Clock` next to overdue Task due dates.
- EC42: the hint under "Alasan".
- E1-S4: "Funding Officer nonaktif" label.

## Accessibility notes

- Each checkbox's label is the Task name; the due date and "Terlambat" are in its description.
- The stepper is an ordered list with `aria-current="step"` on the current Stage.
- Ticking updates optimistically and announces "Siapkan materi selesai" in a polite live region.
