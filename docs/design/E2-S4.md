# E2-S4 Prospect detail

## Task

The Admin sees everything about one Prospect (who to call, where its Pipeline stands, what visits are planned) and acts on it: start a Pipeline, plan a Kegiatan, archive.

## Priority of content

1. Name, Stage of the open Pipeline, Funding Officer
2. The open Pipeline (link) or "Mulai Pipeline"
3. Upcoming Kegiatan, then "Tambah Kegiatan"
4. Kontak with tap-to-call
5. Data: Kategori, Alamat, Lokasi, Potensi rekening, Catatan
6. Closed Pipelines, past Kegiatan
7. Ubah, Arsipkan

## States

- **No open Pipeline:** the Pipeline section reads "Belum ada Pipeline berjalan." with "Mulai Pipeline" (a template select appears in a dialog / sheet, defaulting to the only active template).
- **No Kegiatan:** "Belum ada Kegiatan untuk Prospect ini." with "Tambah Kegiatan".
- **Loading:** section skeletons.
- **Error:** "Data Prospect gagal dimuat." with "Coba lagi".
- **Not found (EC40):** "Prospect tidak ditemukan. Mungkin alamatnya salah." with "Kembali ke daftar Prospect".
- **Archived (EC40):** a muted banner at the top: "Prospect ini diarsipkan pada 12 Nov 2026." with "Pulihkan". All other actions are hidden.

## Edge cases

- EC16: "Mulai Pipeline" shows only with no open or On Hold Pipeline.
- EC19: with an open Pipeline, the "Arsipkan" menu item is replaced by a disabled item with the reason "Tutup atau tunda Pipeline dulu." With no open Pipeline, "Arsipkan" opens a confirm: "Arsipkan SDN 3 Cibinong? 2 Kegiatan mendatang akan dibatalkan." (count from the archive preview; the sentence is omitted when the count is 0).
- E1-S4: "Funding Officer nonaktif" next to the name.
- EC34: no location: "Belum ada lokasi" with "Tambah lokasi" (opens the edit form at the Lokasi section).
