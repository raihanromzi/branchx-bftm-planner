## Code review

No findings. Creating an Activity takes FOR SHARE on the Prospect, and archiving takes FOR UPDATE, so no Activity can be added to a Prospect while it is being archived. Cancel locks its row and checks the version. All SQL goes through Drizzle's parameterised `sql`.

Builder decisions accepted, because they are within the criteria:
- Any active Funding Officer can be assigned to an Activity. The form defaults to the Prospect's (E4-S2 #2).
- A linked Pipeline must be the Prospect's open one. On Hold is refused.
- "Future" means starting after now. Overdue Scheduled Activities are not cancelled by an archive.
- Restoring a Prospect does not bring back cancelled Activities. A visit is planned again as a new Activity (EC27).

`tests/unit/E2-S1.test.ts`: the route count in the no-DELETE check went from 4 to 5 for `archive-preview`. This is a test-only change.

ESLint scanned the generated `playwright-report/`, so lint failed. The fix is to add `playwright-report/**` and `test-results/**` to the ESLint ignores.
