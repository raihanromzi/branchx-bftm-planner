## Bugs

### BUG-2: focus lost after a server field error

Found by CI on push `cd61e27`. The test failed on mobile twice and was flaky on desktop. It passed locally.

- **Story and criterion:** E2-S3, criterion 1 (server errors appear under their fields, and focus moves there).
- **Steps:** open the form, choose a Funding Officer, deactivate that Funding Officer elsewhere, then press Simpan.
- **Expected:** "Funding Officer ini sudah nonaktif." under Funding Officer, and the select is focused.
- **Actual:** the message showed, but focus stayed nowhere.
- **Cause:** the selects are `disabled` while saving. `focusField` focused in `requestAnimationFrame`, which can run before React commits the render that ends `pending`. A disabled select cannot take focus, so on a slower machine the focus was dropped. Keyboard and screen-reader users lost their place.
- **Fix:** `focusField` now records a focus request in state, and an effect focuses the field after that commit (`src/components/prospects/prospect-form-screen.tsx`).
- **Verification:** the test run 10 times per viewport with 6 workers, 30 of 30 passed; the full E2-S3 spec, 57 of 57; lint and typecheck clean; CI green on the fix commit. Status: Verified.
