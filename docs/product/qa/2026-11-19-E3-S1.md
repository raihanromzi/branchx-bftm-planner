## Code review

No open findings. Accepted decisions:
- Names are unique among active templates (a partial unique index; 409 `template_name_taken`).
- PUT replaces the whole template, deleting and reinserting Stages and Tasks. Pipelines copy Stages and Tasks, so nothing refers to their ids.
- 422 `last_template` refuses archiving or deleting the only active template, under an advisory lock.
- `pipelineCounts` is the single hook E3-S3 completes.

Corrected in review: the seed comment and `api.md` called the default Stages an open question. They were approved at the PRD gate.
